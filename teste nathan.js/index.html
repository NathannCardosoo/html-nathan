Neymar Carrer

The beginning of everything

Neymar da Silva Santos Júnior was born on February 5, 1992, in Mogi das Cruzes, São Paulo, Brazil. Since childhood, he loved playing football and dreamed of becoming a professional player. His father, Neymar Santos Sr., supported him and helped him develop his talent.

The Beginning of His Career

At the age of 11, Neymar joined Santos FC's youth academy. In 2009, at just 17 years old, he made his professional debut. He quickly became one of Brazil's biggest football stars because of his speed, dribbling, and incredible skills.

Success at Santos

Between 2010 and 2013, Neymar won important titles with Santos, including the Copa Libertadores in 2011. His performances attracted the attention of major European clubs.
